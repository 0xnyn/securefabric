---
title: "Manage your team"
description: "Invite teammates to the dashboard, change their roles, and remove them."
---

Your team is shared across your account's environments. Each member has one [role](/dashboard/roles). Only admins can change the team.

## Invite a teammate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the team settings">
    In the dashboard, open **Settings** and select the **Team** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Select **Invite**, enter your teammate's email address, and choose a role: **Admin**, **Developer** or **Support**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your teammate accepts">
    They receive an email invitation. After they sign in, they appear in the member list with the role you chose.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change a role or remove a member

On the **Team** tab, change a member's role or remove them. Changes apply to the member's next request.

Every invitation, role change and removal is recorded in the [audit log](/dashboard/audit-log) as `member.invited`, `member.role_changed` or `member.removed`.

## Who can see the team

Every member can see the member list. Only admins can invite, change or remove members; others get `403 role_required`.

<Note>
  Team management is a dashboard feature. API keys can't manage members.
</Note>

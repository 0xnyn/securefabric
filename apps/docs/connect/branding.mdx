---
title: "Brand the connect UI"
description: "Show your name, logo and colors on the hosted connect page."
---

The connect UI can carry your brand, so your customers see your product rather than SecureFabric's. Branding is set per [environment](/concepts/environments).

## Set your branding

Admins set branding in the dashboard under **Settings**, on the **Connect page** tab:

| Setting | Effect | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Display name | The name at the top of the page. | The environment's name |
| Logo URL | An HTTPS image shown next to the name. | None |
| Accent colour | The color of buttons and highlights. | `#4f46e5` |
| Theme | `system` follows the visitor's setting; `light` or `dark` forces one. | `system` |

The settings apply to every new and open connect session in the environment.

## Set allowed redirect origins

On the **General** tab of **Settings**, list the origins the connect UI may redirect to after the flow, such as `https://app.example.com`. See [Redirect after the flow](/connect/overview#redirect-after-the-flow).

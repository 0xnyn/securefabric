---
title: "Re-authorize a connection"
description: "Fix expired credentials or missing permissions without losing the connection's ID or data."
---

Re-authorize a connection when its [health](/concepts/connection-health) is `UNAUTHORIZED` or `REAUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`, when your customer uninstalled the GitHub App, or when they need to grant new permissions. The connection keeps its ID, history and data.

## Send your customer a re-authorization link

Create a connect session with the connection's `connection_id`:

```bash
curl https://api.securefabric.dev/v1/connect_sessions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $SF_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)" \
  -d '{"organization_id": "'"$ORG_ID"'", "connection_id": "'"$CONNECTION_ID"'"}'
```

The session offers only the connection's integration. Open the `url` with [connect-js](/connect/embed) or send it to your customer.

- **Form connectors**: your customer enters new credentials. SecureFabric verifies them before switching; if they are rejected, the old credentials stay in place.
- **Redirect connectors** such as GitHub: your customer goes through the vendor's authorization again.

The `connection_id` must belong to `organization_id`, otherwise the request fails with `404 connection_not_found`.

## Rotate credentials yourself

If you hold the credentials for a form connector, send them directly:

```bash
curl -X PATCH https://api.securefabric.dev/v1/connections/$CONNECTION_ID \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $SF_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"credentials": {"api_key": "sbx_new_key"}}'
```

The same verification applies. See [Rotate credentials](/concepts/connections#rotate-credentials).

## From the dashboard

On a connection's page in the dashboard, create a re-authorization link and copy it for your customer.
